---

## What "Winner Shape" Means for Abu

- Winners are NOT "generic app ported to chain X." They are **chain-native** builds that couldn't exist elsewhere.
- The strongest wedge is something that uses Portaldot's specific primitives (contracts, multisig, identity pallet) in a way that the AI layer makes uniquely useful.
- 4-day builds are fine — dAppForge was a hackathon project. Execution quality + demo quality matters more than feature completeness.
- **Demo = demo day gate.** A clean, end-to-end live demo of one meaningful thing beats a half-working 5-feature project.
