## Tracks (4 total)

### 1. Native Onchain Apps
Build user-facing onchain products with clear use cases and runnable MVPs. Focus on native Portaldot architecture (not EVM ports). Examples: consumer apps, payment flows, mini dApps, community products, creator tools, membership systems.

**Looking for:** Clear user scenario · Runnable MVP · Real Portaldot integration · Strong end-to-end demo

### 2. Builder Tools for Portaldot
Dev tooling that improves the Portaldot developer experience. CLIs, explorers, monitoring, testing utilities, deployment scripts, debuggers, doc helpers, workflow automation.

**Looking for:** Real developer pain point · Useful tool (even lightweight) · Demo showing real workflow improvement · Strong Portaldot relevance

### 3. Onchain Identity & Coordination
Identity, reputation, credentials, access control, community coordination on Portaldot. Onchain profiles, badges, contribution records, attendance proofs, role-based access, membership systems.

**Looking for:** Clear identity/coordination problem · Simple convincing product flow · Runnable MVP with real onchain value · Native Portaldot capabilities

### 4. AI-Powered Onchain Workflows
AI or automation-driven workflows on Portaldot. AI copilots, transaction assistants, workflow agents, chain data analysis, monitoring/alerts, products combining AI interfaces with Portaldot execution.

**Looking for:** Real workflow improved by AI/automation · Clear product logic and scope · Runnable MVP with useful demo · Honest AI + onchain integration

## Red Flags & Notable Observations

1. **Zero submissions with 4 days left.** 189 registered hackers, 0 submissions. Either submissions are coming in a last-minute rush (common in short hackathons) or participation rate will be very low. This is a significant opportunity — field may be thin at close.

2. **Per-prize USDT split undisclosed.** Total pool is 3,500 USDT but no 1st/2nd/3rd/track breakdown. Could mean judges have discretion or organizers simply haven't published it. Low transparency here.

3. **No judges listed.** Anonymous judging panel is a minor concern for credibility, but not unusual for small ecosystem hackathons.

4. **No Community Favorite Award mechanism explained.** Community voting is mentioned but no voting link, format, or timing is described.

5. **Substrate/Rust-only, no EVM.** Hard constraint. Any team that ports EVM contracts will likely fail the mandatory eligibility gate. Strong moat for teams that know Rust/ink!.

6. **Portaldot ecosystem is small.** No external sponsors, no grants from major chains. This is a first-season event — organizer track record is unknown. Grants/bounties promised post-hackathon ("within the PortalDot ecosystem") but nothing contractually binding listed.

7. **Demo Day = deadline day (May 31).** No gap between submission close and demo. Implies live demos are scheduled same day as final submission, which is aggressive.

8. **Internshala listing language is AI-generated slop** (e.g. "global developer platform," "bypass bureaucratic competition bottlenecks"). Not organic community excitement — just SEO aggregator. Organic interest signal is low.
